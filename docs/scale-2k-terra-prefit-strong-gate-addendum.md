# scale-2k Terra prefit strong-evidence addendum

Date: 2026-08-23.

## Authority and purpose

Hugh directed the campaign to review the failing plan, switch new semantic extraction to Terra, and keep going toward 1,000 reviewed packages. This addendum corrects one remaining process defect exposed by Batch 18. It does not lower or replace any electrical, provenance, citation, hard-bound, collision, package, independent-review, promotion, deployment, or publishing gate.

## Reproduced defect

The recovery authorization stopped before fitting only when fewer than 20 of 40 candidates passed pure evidence preflight. Its unchanged final gate also required at least 10 direct typical, curve-calibrated, or genuine typed-interval packages, but it did not test whether that count was reachable before consuming the single fit pass.

Batch 18 sealed 37 preflight-admissible candidates, yet only 7 used strong evidence; the other 30 were maximum-bound-only. Fitting cannot change the provenance class of sealed evidence. A fit pass therefore could never have met the unchanged 10-strong final gate.

## Corrected prefit rule

Every later Terra tranche must satisfy both necessary prefit conditions before fitting:

1. at least 20 of 40 fixed-denominator rows pass the pure factory evidence validator; and
2. at least 10 of 40 rows already have direct typical, curve-calibrated, or genuine typed-interval evidence.

If either condition fails, the tranche stops before fitting and records evidence-source yield. No candidate may be reclassified from a maximum inequality to a typical equality, no fit pass may be spent on a mathematically impossible final gate, and the campaign continues with the next untouched contiguous frozen range under the same thresholds.

Batch 18 therefore ran zero fits and promoted zero packages. The reviewed library remains 737. The next authorized range is the untouched contiguous diode tranche 770 through 809.
