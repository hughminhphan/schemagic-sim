# ADR-0008: Motor gate-driver facts-V3.1 architecture boundaries

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-24

## Context

The frozen `motor.full-bridge-gate-driver` facts-V2 contract requires one reviewed value for every driver regardless of architecture. That shape cannot honestly represent common primary-source devices:

- a driver may expose only floating switch-node voltage limits rather than a motor-bus supply-pin range;
- gate-drive bias may be externally supplied or generated by an internal regulator;
- timing may be bounded by minimum input pulse width without a published maximum PWM frequency;
- gate strength may be specified as peak current or output resistance;
- dead time may be fixed, adaptive, programmable, or supplied externally;
- a high-side supply may use bootstrap, charge pump, or bootstrap with charge-pump top-off, without publishing a fixed maximum duty cycle or bootstrap overhead charge;
- current sensing may be absent or may lack one configuration-independent input-voltage limit; and
- local decoupling guidance may be typical, ratio-based, or open-bound rather than a closed minimum.

Treating an inapplicable field as zero, converting current and an assumed interval into charge, promoting typical observations to hard limits, or relabeling a switch-node rating as a supply-pin range would manufacture engineering claims. Changing facts-V2 or facts `3.0.0` in place would break frozen profile bytes, codecs, admission records, and recipe identities.

## Decision

Add exact facts schema `3.1.0` only for `motor.full-bridge-gate-driver`.

The outer profile envelope remains `schemaVersion: "1.0.0"`. Facts `3.1.0` does not accept the MOSFET or supply-TVS classes already defined by facts `3.0.0`. V1, facts-V2, and facts `3.0.0` schemas, codecs, canonical bytes, content hashes, admission records, release contents, and recipe identities remain unchanged. Any recipe that consumes a facts `3.1.0` gate-driver profile must have a new immutable identity and bind the exact `(partClass, factsSchemaVersion)` codec.

The facts `3.1.0` gate-driver record is closed and flat. Every field is present. Architecture discriminators are reviewed facts; fields inactive for the selected branch are explicit unknowns rather than omitted, zero-filled, calculated, or estimated values.

### Architecture-conditioned facts

Bridge voltage:

- `bridgeVoltageInterface` distinguishes `motor_bus_supply_pin` from `switch_node_only`;
- reviewed operating minimum, operating maximum, and absolute maximum retain the selected interface's published meaning;
- the operating minimum permits a signed switch-node limit;
- a recipe may compare requested motor-supply minimum only for `motor_bus_supply_pin`. A `switch_node_only` profile cannot create a motor-supply-minimum claim.

Driver bias:

- `driverBiasSource` distinguishes `external_supply` from `internal_regulator`;
- the external branch requires reviewed bias-input minimum and maximum and exact-unknown regulator-output limits;
- the internal branch requires reviewed regulator-output minimum and maximum and exact-unknown external bias-input limits;
- no unconditional scalar `gateVoltage` is carried. Bias-tracking, internally regulated, and configurable outputs cannot be collapsed into one condition-free voltage.

Timing and gate strength:

- `pwmMaximum` and `minimumPulseWidth` are each reviewed or explicit unknown, with at least one reviewed for admission;
- each reviewed timing quantity carries a reviewed `guaranteed_bound` or `typical_observation` role; an unknown quantity has an exact-unknown role;
- a recipe applies only reviewed `guaranteed_bound` timing limits and keeps a typical observation unknown for feasibility; it does not infer an undocumented PWM ceiling;
- each drive direction requires either reviewed peak current or reviewed output resistance; the unused representation remains reviewed source data or explicit unknown;
- `deadTimeControl` distinguishes `fixed`, `adaptive`, `programmable`, and `external` control;
- a reviewed numeric `deadTime` is mandatory only for `fixed`. Other modes may retain a reviewed bound when one exists or explicit unknown when it does not.

High-side supply:

- retain the closed `highSideSupply` values `bootstrap`, `charge_pump`, `bootstrap_with_charge_pump`, and `bootstrap_with_top_off_charge_pump`;
- require a reviewed `continuousHighSideOnSupported` boolean;
- carry `bootstrapMaximumDutyCycle` and `highSideBiasCurrentMaximum` as reviewed source data or explicit unknown;
- remove unconditional `bootstrapAllowedRipple` and `bootstrapOverheadCharge`. Allowed ripple is a design choice, while charge cannot be manufactured from a published current without a source-bounded interval.

Sense and local supply:

- `currentSenseInterface` distinguishes `none`, `amplifier`, and `comparator`;
- `none` requires `senseMaximumVoltage` to be exact unknown; other branches permit reviewed source data or explicit unknown;
- `localDecouplingMinimum` permits reviewed source data or explicit unknown when the manufacturer publishes only typical, ratio-based, or open-bound guidance.

Topology, logic threshold, quiescent current, junction-to-ambient thermal resistance, and maximum junction temperature remain mandatory reviewed facts. Package identity and mounted geometry reuse the facts-V2 geometry contract: a reviewed exact package, a canonically calculated bounding rectangle from evidence-backed manufacturer-recommended land-pattern dimensions or reviewed assembly measurement, and a reviewed maximum mounted height.

### Evidence and admission boundary

All reviewed electrical facts retain the published conditions in the existing `validFor` shape, using the closed facts `3.1.0` condition vocabulary and canonical ordering. Reviewed fact and condition evidence must satisfy the existing manufacturer-source or independent-measurement admission boundary and content-hash requirements.

Conditional and alternative fields accept only `reviewed` or exact `unknown`. An exact unknown has a null value, no evidence, and no conditions. Calculated and estimated electrical facts are not admissible. The sole calculated admission surface remains mounted board area, whose arithmetic and evidence union are deterministically checked from reviewed source dimensions.

Validation deterministically enforces:

- the closed profile and fact key sets;
- discriminator values and active/inactive branch states;
- timing and gate-capability alternative groups;
- ordered voltage and bias pairs;
- numeric domains, units, canonical condition order, and condition coverage;
- primary evidence provenance; and
- the existing mounted-geometry arithmetic and evidence rules.

## Consequences

- Real full-bridge gate drivers can be admitted without inventing inapplicable bias, bootstrap, dead-time, timing, gate-strength, or sense facts.
- Admission establishes that the exact part profile is structurally valid, source-backed, and eligible for exact-version selection. It does not prove that a requested motor operating point is feasible.
- Unknown or condition-mismatched facts remain unknown in recipe checks; admission never turns them into passing design constraints.
- Gate-network adequacy, bootstrap capacitance, bias-supply implementation, current-limit behavior, switching and conduction loss, thermal performance, transient coordination, PCB placement, routing, and complete system safety remain outside profile admission.
- A selected exact part has no implied executable model. Simulation coverage remains explicitly unavailable unless a separately reviewed selected-part model and its applicability are bound by a future contract.
- Mounted board area remains a deterministic ranking proxy, not a PCB-fit or layout proof.
