Voltage ramp

Isoluminant diverging ramp, OKLCH L fixed at 0.62. Hue carries sign (245 negative, 62 positive), chroma carries magnitude with gamma 1.6 so mid voltages stay muted and only the rails carry real colour. Interpolated in OKLCH, never in sRGB. Clamps at the rails.

Schematic canvas (Vellum)

Solid is positive, dashed 8-4 is negative, hairline plus ground glyph is within 50 mV of ground. Always on, never toggleable, survives full achromatopsia.

Scope well (Graphite 900)

Hot variants are permitted only here: scope traces and pulse cores on a Graphite surface. Never on Vellum, never in UI.

Current encoding

Current is never given a hue. Hue is spent on voltage; current is motion, which makes it colourblind safe by construction. Under prefers-reduced-motion the same information moves to stroke width and static chevrons, and that static form is byte for byte the SVG export encoding.

Animated: pulse density and speed

Top wire is at the 1 µA floor: nothing moves. The absence of motion means the net is not conducting, and that is load bearing.

Reduced motion and export

Stroke width ladder 0.9 / 1.4 / 2.0 / 2.8 px carries magnitude, chevron direction carries current direction, chevron spacing repeats the pulse period. No animation, same information.

Operating point

2N3904 NPN small signal transistor in TO-92. Model fidelity F2: Gummel Poon parameters fitted to the manufacturer datasheet curves at 25 °C. Beta and saturation voltage are trustworthy; switching times are not characterised.

Self hosting requirement

This specimen loads fonts from Google Fonts. The application must not. Ship subset woff2 files in repo, served from the same origin, with an explicit font-display policy. Archivo Expanded is subset to the wordmark glyph set only and must land under 8 KB, otherwise it is cut and the wordmark falls back to IBM Plex Sans 600 at 0.08em tracking. A CDN font link in the shipped app is a hard ban.

Hard bans, short form

  • No glow, gradient, shadow or blur that is not data. Only three gradients exist: the voltage ramp, the LED photometric falloff, scope trace persistence. Zero elevation anywhere.
  • No pills, chips, cards or rounded rectangles. Radius 0 everywhere except 2 px on text inputs and the pot knob.
  • No semantic hue as text colour. Text is the Graphite ramp on Vellum, or Vellum on Graphite.
  • No purple. No hero, no onboarding modal, no entrance animation. No blueprint grid, texture, grain or noise.
  • No generic icon where an IEC 60617 symbol exists. No rounded caps or joins. No seven segment typeface. No CDN fonts.
  • No dashboard vocabulary. Instrument words only. No fabricated values on non convergence. No em dashes in any copy.
